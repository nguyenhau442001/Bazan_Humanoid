## UBECs

One 6 V 8 A UBEC per leg; the arms and neck share the left leg's. Sized for a single fault: one branch at its limit (a leg at the 6 A trip, or the arms at their 4 A fuse) while the rest walk: worst 6.6 A per UBEC. One UBEC for everything would see 8.8 A in the same fault, over its 8 A rating. Walking alone needs 3.50 A in all.
