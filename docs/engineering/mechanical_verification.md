# Mechanical verification: Bazan BZN-01

Results of the automated checks on the printable CAD (`hardware/mechanical/`), run on 2026-11-15 after the two
geometry corrections described in [mechanical_audit.md](mechanical_audit.md). Angles are in the left-side
convention of the CAD (the right side is the mirror); "twin" means the URDF / MuJoCo / firmware model, which at the
time was generated by `gen_robot.py` (a separate 303 mm model). Since 2026-11-19 the twin is `bazan_cad.urdf`,
generated from this CAD, so the twin-vs-CAD differences reported below are closed.

**Scope of every result below: CAD geometry and arithmetic.** No part was printed, no servo powered. Section 8 lists
the bench tests that the CAD cannot replace.

## 1. How to reproduce

```bash
pip install cadquery matplotlib          # Python 3.11+
cd hardware/mechanical
python check.py                          # leg: 86 poses, slide-out, tool access, both legs
python robot.py                          # whole frame: static fit, 40 sweep poses, tool access, slide-out
python shell.py --quick --no-export      # shell against the frame, 26 poses
python audit.py                          # everything in sections 2 to 7; writes out/audit.json
python audit.py twin fits walls          # single sections
```

## 8. Structural observations

Hand estimates at MG92B stall (0.343 N·m), no safety factor, no test:

| Interface | Load | Stress or margin | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Horn to plate, 2 × ST2×4 at r = 7 mm | 24.5 N per screw in shear | 5.1 MPa bearing on 2.0 × 2.4 mm of PLA | Low; thread stripping on re-assembly is the likelier failure |
| Servo ears, 2 × M2 at 27.8 mm | 12.3 N per screw if the pocket walls carried nothing | – | Low; the pocket walls carry it |
| Thrust ring face, r 13 to 21 mm | About 7 N (everything above one hip in single support) | 0.01 MPa | Negligible; wear and friction matter, not stress |
| Stance hip roll | 0.288 N·m to hold (§5) | 84 % of stall | The servo, not the plastic, is the weak link |
| Ear insert, 0.7 mm to the pocket | Pull-out under ear-screw torque | Not estimated | Test T2 |
| Spine of thigh and shin, across layers | Spreading of the plates | Not estimated | Test T4 |

Not assessed: fatigue, impact from a fall, creep of PLA next to a warm servo, stiffness of the leg (deflection under
load changes foot placement).

## 9. Physical tests before the whole robot is printed

| # | Test | Method | Pass when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| T1 | Measure the bought parts | Calipers on one MG92B, MG90S, SG90, one horn, one plug, each board; compare with `hardware.py`, `electronics.py` | Every MEASURE value within 0.2 mm, or the file updated and prints regenerated |
| T2 | Pocket coupon | Print `L_hip_block`. Press 4 ear inserts and 2 idler inserts. Fit two servos with plugs on | Plug passes each window flat; servo seats on its ears by hand; no bulge into the pocket; an M2×6 torqued snug 10 times does not turn the insert; idler insert face 1.3 ± 0.2 mm proud |
| T3 | Joint coupon | Print `L_shin`. Fit two horns (ST2×4), two bearings, caps; mount on the hip block's pitch servo | Horn drops into its recess; ST2 does not mark the outer face; bearing spins after pressing; plate turns ±45° by hand with no rub; 0.8 mm gap visible both sides |
| T4 | One leg | Print and assemble one leg to `leg_v3.md`. Squeeze and spread the thigh and shin plates by hand before fitting | Assembled without filing; no crack at a spine; time and any screw that could not be reached recorded |
| T5 | Range and leads | Drive each joint slowly to the CAD range (hip roll ±20, ankle roll ±20, others as §2), then the gait, caps off | No contact, no lead taut or pinched at any limit; note the angle of first contact beyond the range |
| T6 | Hip-roll thermal | The P0 test of `hardware_architecture.md`, with the continuous load raised from 1.07 kg·cm (the twin) to 2.9 kg·cm (0.29 N·m, §5): 145 g at 20 mm on the lever | P0's own criteria: position error within 3° and case below 60 °C for 10 min. A fail means decisions 1 and 3 of the audit before any more printing |
| T7 | Torso and arms | Print the frame; fit boards, both arms, head | Every board on its sled; an L key reaches the leg-module screws; all leads and extensions fit inside the shell; arms and head sweep their ranges |
| T8 | Whole robot | Weigh each part and the robot; balance on a knife edge for the CoM height | Mass and CoM entered in the mass model; within 10 % of §5, or the estimate recipe corrected |

Print the coupons first, one leg second, the rest only after T5 and T6.
